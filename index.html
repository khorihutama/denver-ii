TumbuhKembang — Child Development Screening

An unofficial screening aid based on Denver II–style milestones. Screen a child aged 0–6 years across four areas — Personal-Social, Fine Motor-Adaptive, Language, and Gross Motor. Enter the birth date, mark what the child can do, and get a Normal / Suspect / Untestable result.

Unofficial aid — this is NOT the official Denver II test, and this project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Denver Developmental Materials, Inc. or the test authors. Screening only, not a diagnosis: the official test uses the official form and is administered and interpreted by trained professionals, and the milestone ages here are a simplified approximation. Do not use this app for clinical decisions — if a result worries you, discuss it with a pediatrician.

How scoring works: a Delay is a failed item that 90% of children pass at a younger age (bar fully left of the age line). A Caution is a failed item whose 75–90% range crosses the age line. Suspect = 1 or more Delays, or 2 or more Cautions. Untestable = refusals land where a Delay or Cautions would have been scored. Method based on the Denver Developmental Screening Test II (Frankenburg et al.); this is an unofficial project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the test authors or publisher, and not for clinical use.
